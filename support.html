FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Grand Theft Auto VI is officially scheduled to release on November 19, 2026. Pre-loading will become available for digital pre-orders on November 10, 2026.

At launch, Grand Theft Auto VI will be available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Details regarding a PC release will be shared at a later date.

Pre-order bonuses are tied directly to your linked Rockstar Games Social Club account. Please ensure the platform account (PSN or Xbox Live) you used to purchase the game is linked to your active Social Club profile.

Log in to the Rockstar Games Social Club website, navigate to Settings > Linked Accounts, and follow the on-screen prompts for your respective console network.
